General

23-year-old software enthusiast, terrible ceramacist, unhinged optimist, and Vermonter in Minnesota.

Likes: bikes, hikes, my Linux dotfiles, fermentation, words, the smell of the mist in the woods after rain, NASA, hanging out on ridges in New Hampshire wrapped in space blankets, skis, David Foster Wallace unedited Interview (2003) on YouTube, Cormac McCarthy, Kentucky Bluegrass (the music), deer season, long showers, anything by Octavia Butler, four tet, merino wool, and my street in Saint Paul.

Dislikes: lukewarm baths, Santal 33, John Updike, Kentucky bluegrass (the grass), industrial animal farming, George R. R. Martin delaying The Winds of Winter AGAIN (come on dude), and blue light after ~6 pm.

Professional

I enjoy technical and interpersonal work with opportunities for learning and growth. I love solving mysteries where many systems come together and interact in unpredictable ways. I also enjoy networking, and have experience with user-and-process-focused business solutions in engineering, manufacturing, scientific, and health science R&D settings on Cisco and HPE (Aruba) equipment. I love working with people to deeply understand their workflows and design thoughful sysems that help them do their best work while meeting technical requirements.

Mission Statement:
