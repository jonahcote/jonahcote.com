This site is set in CMU Serif Roman, from Donald Knuth's Computer Modern type family. Certain headers are set in CMU Concrete from the same collection.

All the elements of the site were coded by hand. Eventually, I'd like to make a simple static site generator to allow me to write journal entries in markdown.

The photo on the front page is of Cueva de las Monos. The art was started nearly 10,000 years ago! The image itself is uncredited and was released to the public domain.

The contact photos were shot by my friend Murphy with Lomochrome Purple 400 film on a Nikon F3.
